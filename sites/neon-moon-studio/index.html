Victoria keeps original sketches simple in pencil so a design can be tattooed in black and grey, fine line, realism, color, or something in between. If one appeals to you, describe it to the shop — a short description or a screenshot is how she asks people to claim a design. She talks through the final look, then which size to book.

03 The artist

Hi, I’m Victoria.

I graduated from Albright College in Reading in 2010 with a BA in Fine Arts, painting and illustration. After that I worked as a freelance graphic and web designer, a canvas painter and mural artist, and an acrylic painting instructor across Berks County. I was a tattoo collector before I became a tattoo artist.

In 2020, during the early pandemic, I was offered an apprenticeship and trained under a mentor in Boyertown, PA, focusing on realism in black and grey and in color. By the end of that year I wanted a private, welcoming studio where I could connect more personally with clients. I opened Neon Moon Tattoo in January 2021, a cozy studio in the Flying Hills Shopping Center.

Flying Hills has always felt like home. I grew up here, chalking the sidewalks and sketching in the grassy fields. That same spirit is with me every day I come to work.

I specialize in animal and pet portrait tattoos — realistic pieces in color and in black and grey that hold personality, emotion, and the bond people share with the animals they love. Pets, wildlife, birds, sea creatures, and insects. I also make nature-inspired designs and other meaningful tattoos in color and in black and grey.

Thank you for trusting me with your skin, your stories, and your referrals.

Common questions

Do you take walk-ins?

No. Appointment only. No same-day tattoos. Tuesday through Saturday, by appointment. Closed Sunday and Monday.

Who does the tattooing?

Victoria is the owner and the only tattoo artist. She opened the studio in January 2021.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Pricing is a flat rate based on size and color. The total is shown in the studio’s official online booking. The deposit is $100, non-refundable, and applied to that total. This preview does not include a full price list.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. Clients must be 18 or older.

Do you offer piercings?

No. Piercings, permanent makeup, and body jewelry are not offered.

What is a Want To Do tattoo?

A pre-drawn original design. Sketches stay simple so they can be finished in black and grey, linework, or color. Describe the design to the shop before booking. Availability changes, and this preview does not show which designs are currently open.

Where do I go when I arrive?

20 Village Center Dr., 2nd floor, through the black gate. The studio is in the Flying Hills Shopping Center, in the front lot facing Route 10, Reading, PA 19607.
